Before you reach out.

Can I start with a leak or a small repair?+

Yes. SCG lists roof inspection and repair. Tell us what you’ve noticed and when it began; you don’t need to diagnose the cause first.

Do you also handle gutters and attic ventilation?+

SCG’s current website lists gutter installation and cleaning, attic ventilation, and skylight work alongside roofing services. Choose the service that fits your question.

Where is SCG based?+

SCG publishes a Hooksett, New Hampshire address and describes its service region as Southern New Hampshire. Your exact location and scheduling still need confirmation.

Can I include photos?+

Yes. Add up to three JPG, PNG or WebP images to a demo inquiry. Please use sample photos without faces, addresses or other private information. Images are stored privately for the demo owner.

Can I get a price or book an inspection here?+

You can try a sample inspection request or open Tiberius’s live demo calendar. A booking on that calendar is with Tiberius, not SCG Roofing. Roofing prices and SCG appointments must be confirmed directly with the business.

Can I use a simple form?+

Yes. The request form works without AI usage. Demo inquiries go to Tiberius, not SCG Roofing.

Is this SCG’s live website?+

No. This is an independent Tiberius demonstration. The AI answers roofing questions, requests are saved for the demo owner, and the booking calendar is Michael’s Tiberius calendar. This site does not contact or schedule SCG Roofing.
